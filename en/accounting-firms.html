AI automation for accounting firms

Between filing deadlines and seasonal peak loads, accounting firms have a lot to gain from automating repetitive admin tasks.

Why this matters

More time for advisory work, less for data entry

A large share of an accounting firm's work is repetitive and rule-based: recording documents, reconciling data, keeping clients informed of deadlines. AI automation speeds up exactly these tasks, so your team can focus on professional, advisory work. This is one of the most common applications of our AI Automation service.

Concrete examples

What we can automate for you

Automatic document processing

Incoming invoices and documents get recognized and categorized automatically using AI.

Fast client query responses

A chatbot answers common client questions (missing documents, status) around the clock.

Deadline reminders for clients

Automated reminders about filing and payment deadlines reduce late submissions.

Automatic status reports

Clients regularly and automatically receive an overview of their current status.

Cross-system data sync

Data transfer between your accounting software and other tools (banking, online stores, CRM) is automated.

New client onboarding

Onboarding new clients and requesting the necessary documents can be sped up with templates and AI.
