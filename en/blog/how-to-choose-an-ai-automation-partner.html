How to Choose an AI Automation Partner: 8 Things to Check Before You Sign

What questions should you ask an AI automation partner before signing a contract? 8 practical criteria to help you avoid a bad decision.

The AI automation market is growing fast, and so is the number of providers. But it matters a great deal who you trust with your business processes. The eight criteria below will help you objectively evaluate a potential partner before making a decision.

1. Do they actually understand your industry?

A good partner doesn't try to force a universal template onto your business — they ask questions: what processes do you have, where's the biggest pain point? If the first conversation is only about the technology and not about your problems, that's a warning sign.

2. Do they provide a written, itemized quote?

A reliable provider gives you a concrete, priced, written proposal after the assessment — not a verbal, "roughly" estimate. That way you know exactly what you're getting and for how much.

3. Do they have a clear implementation process?

Ask: what does the project look like from the first conversation to launch? An experienced partner has a clear, step-by-step answer to this — assessment, plan, implementation, support.

4. Do they communicate clearly, or just in jargon?

If a partner uses technical terms in every sentence without explaining what they mean, working with them long-term will be difficult. Clear communication isn't a luxury — it's a basic requirement.

5. Do they offer support after launch?

AI automation isn't a one-time project — systems need maintenance and fine-tuning. Ask what support options are available after launch.

6. Do they set realistic expectations, or promise miracles?

Be wary of anyone promising "100% automation" or guaranteed, instant results. Reliable partners set realistic, measurable goals and speak honestly about limitations too.

7. How scalable is their solution?

If your business grows, the system you implement needs to scale with it. Ask how they handle growing data volumes or expanding processes.

8. Do they have references or concrete examples from similar businesses?

While every company is unique, an experienced partner can point to concrete examples from businesses of a similar size or industry — whether that's SMEs, online stores, or accounting firms.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it worth getting multiple quotes?
Yes, definitely. Comparing one or two alternative proposals helps you get a realistic sense of pricing and differences in approach.

What does it mean when a partner offers a "free audit"?
It means they assess your processes without obligation before recommending any paid service — so you get a picture of the possibilities with no risk.

How quickly should I expect a response to an inquiry?
A professionally run partner responds to an inquiry within 1-2 business days.
