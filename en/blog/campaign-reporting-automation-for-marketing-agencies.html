Marketing Agencies: Automating Campaign Reports with AI

Does putting together campaign reports take hours every month? See how AI can automate reporting, and how much time it frees up.

At a marketing agency, the start of the month often looks the same: gathering data from various ad platforms, organizing it into spreadsheets, then assembling client-by-client reports. For many agencies, this process eats up days every month — yet most of it can be automated.

Why does report creation take so long?

For a typical client, campaign data lives across multiple platforms — Google Ads, Meta Ads, possibly other ad managers — and it has to be manually collected, standardized, and turned into something clear and client-ready. The more clients an agency has, the more this work multiplies.

How does AI automation help?

Automatic data aggregation. Data from ad platforms automatically comes together into clear, client-ready reports — no manual exporting and merging.

AI-generated written summaries. Alongside the raw numbers, AI can automatically generate a short, clear summary of what happened in the campaign and why, which also speeds up client communication.

Content and ad copy variants. The copy variations needed for A/B testing can be produced quickly with AI, freeing up time for strategic work.

Client onboarding and briefing automation. Onboarding new clients and the briefing process can be significantly sped up with templates and AI.

You can read more about these on our solution for marketing agencies page.

What does the agency gain from this?

The biggest win isn't just time saved — it's consistency: automatically generated reports always deliver the same format and quality, regardless of who's putting them together or how packed the month is.

What to watch out for during implementation

It's important that automated reporting doesn't become impersonal. The goal isn't to remove people from the process entirely, but to let colleagues spend their time on analysis and client relationships instead of raw data collection.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the automation work with every ad platform?
Implementation adapts to the platforms you actually use — during the assessment, we map out exactly which systems need to be connected.

Does automatic reporting mean losing a personal touch?
No, not if it's well designed. The template and the AI-generated summary can be tailored to the agency's and client's tone, while the underlying data stays accurate and consistent.

Is this worth it for smaller agencies too?
Yes — in fact, it's especially noticeable at a smaller team when they save multiple days a month on report creation.
