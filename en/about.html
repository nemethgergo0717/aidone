About Us

We believe AI and a good website are essential today

We don't force one-size-fits-all solutions — every business is at a different stage, and needs smarter technology in different ways.

Our Mission

Simpler technology, clearer communication

AI Done exists so that AI and modern web solutions aren't just for large corporations. Whether you're running a small online store or a more complex B2B company, our goal is the same: help in plain language, with measurable results.

The Founder

Gergő Németh — from strategic planning to AI automation

AI Done Kft. was founded by Gergő Németh, who has spent close to two decades working in strategic planning and systems design at multinational companies. Along the way, he learned that even the best technology only matters when there's clear business logic, a well-thought-out process, and a measurable goal behind it — not the other way around.

AI Done Kft. grew out of that mindset: instead of forcing AI onto a business, we first understand where the real bottlenecks are, then build the automation — and, where needed, the website — that actually solves them. The experience gained in international corporate environments helps us work with small and medium-sized businesses in a structured way, with a transparent process and a realistic timeline, not just with large enterprise clients.
